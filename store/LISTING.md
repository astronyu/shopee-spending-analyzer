# Chrome Web Store listing

## Name

Shopee Spending Analyzer

## Short description

Understand your Shopee spending with local Excel reports: purchase summaries, order details, item breakdowns and spending trends.

## Detailed description

Where does your Shopee spending go? Turn your purchase history into an Excel report you can explore, filter and keep.

Use your existing signed-in Shopee session, start a scan and download four useful worksheets:

SUMMARY — YOUR SPENDING AT A GLANCE
See total spending, paid orders, purchased items, average and largest order, captured savings and shipping, your top shop and available purchase dates.

ORDERS — A CLEAR PURCHASE LEDGER
Review order numbers, dates, sellers, amounts paid, statuses and available shipping, voucher and coin breakdowns. Filter orders in Excel and see which contribute to spending. Follow links back to sellers.

ITEMS — THE DETAILS BEHIND EACH PURCHASE
Find products, variations, quantities, recorded original and paid prices, subtotals and returned flags for orders included in spending. Revisit products and sellers through workbook links.

ANALYTICS — DISCOVER YOUR SHOPPING PATTERNS
Compare yearly and monthly spending, order counts, items, discounts and captured shipping. See shops ranked by spending. Use exported values to create your own Excel charts or budget analysis.

LOCAL PROCESSING
No separate account or password entry. No developer reporting server, advertising or analytics. Requests go directly to Shopee using your existing session; analysis and Excel generation happen locally. You choose when to save a workbook.

MARKET SUPPORT
Malaysia was validated during development. Singapore, Philippines, Indonesia, Thailand, Vietnam, Taiwan and Brazil are supported by design, but have not been individually validated. Regional order formats may differ.

PLEASE NOTE
Shopee's internal APIs can change. Missing shipping or voucher fields may appear blank or zero, and some discounts are derived from available totals. Cancelled, unpaid and return/refund statuses are excluded as mapped by this version. This is not a refund reconciliation tool. Large histories may reach pagination limits. Keep the popup open while scanning.

Independent, open-source project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Shopee.
